How to play
🎯 Goal Be the first to land exactly on square 100, the Andromeda Galaxy. Overshoot it and you bounce back for the rest of the roll.
▲ Boosts 🚀 Rockets, 🛸 UFO beams, 🌀 wormholes and ☄️ comets carry you up the board.
▼ Hazards ⚡ Ion storms, 🕳️ black holes, 🔥 flaming asteroids and ☀️ solar flares knock you down.
🎰 Random board Every game deals a new layout of boosts and hazards, with the same number of each.
🎲 Turns Players take turns rolling the 3D dice and moving that many squares. Hold the roll button to charge a harder throw.
🏁 Finishing When someone reaches Andromeda you can keep racing for 2nd, 3rd and the rest, or end and see the results.
🔍 Preview Hover over (or tap) any boost or hazard to see where it leads.
💥 Collisions Land on a rival to knock them back 2 squares.
🔥 Afterburner Rolling a 6 gives you another roll.
🌌 Cosmic events Every 3 rounds: Solar Wind, Gravity Wave, Warp Rift, Meteor Shower or ✨ Galactic Shift, which moves every boost and hazard to a new spot.
🎥 Camera The camera follows the action. Scroll or pinch to zoom and drag to look around; double-click resets.

Keys: hold Space to throw (harder the longer you hold), Enter quick roll · + − zoom · 0 reset view · F full screen
