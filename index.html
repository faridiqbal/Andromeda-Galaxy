How to play
🎯 Goal Be the first to land exactly on square 100, the Andromeda Galaxy. Overshoot it and you bounce back for the rest of the roll.
▲ Boosts 🚀 Rockets, 🛸 UFO beams, 🌀 wormholes and ☄️ comets carry you up the board.
▼ Hazards 🐍 Serpents, 🕳️ black holes, 🪨 asteroids and ☀️ solar flares knock you down. You salvage +1⚡ from each crash.
⚡ Energy Collect 💎 crystals by flying over them (max 5⚡) and spend it on abilities.
🛡️ Shield (2⚡) Before rolling. It blocks the next hazard or collision.
🔺🔻 Thrusters (1⚡ each) After rolling, adjust the move by up to ±2. A landing preview shows where you'll end up.
🎲 Reroll (2⚡) Don't like the roll? Throw again, once per turn.
💥 Collisions Land on a rival to knock them back 2 squares.
🔥 Afterburner Rolling a 6 gives you another roll.
🌌 Cosmic events Every 3 rounds: Solar Wind, Crystal Rain, Ion Storm (free abilities) or Nebula Drain.

Keys: Space roll / launch · ←→ thrusters · S shield · R reroll
